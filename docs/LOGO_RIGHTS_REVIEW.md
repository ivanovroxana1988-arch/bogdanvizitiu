# Logo rights review — portfolio

Updated: 2026-08-13

This is an internal operational checklist, not legal advice. It separates the factual claim that Bogdan worked with an organization from permission to reproduce that organization's visual trademark on a commercial personal-brand website.

## A. Written approval / express licence confirmed by official terms

| Organization | Why it is on the approval list | Official source / action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Amazon | Third-party uses of the Amazon brand outside specifically permitted cases require review and written approval; standalone logo use requires approval. | Amazon Ads brand usage guidelines; request approval through the relevant account/business contact or trademarks channel. |

| PwC | PwC states that its trade names, trademarks, service marks, logos or designs may not be used without the owner's express prior written consent. | PwC Terms and Conditions. |

## B. Request written clearance before public portfolio use

These sources do not give this portfolio page an unrestricted commercial-use licence. Treat them as permission-required before relying on the official media asset.

| Organization | Restriction found | Action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Roche | Official media-library assets are offered to journalists for reporting and must not be used for another purpose. | Ask Roche media/brand team for portfolio-site permission. |
| Schwarz Digits | Schwarz Group press assets are explicitly limited to editorial use. | Ask press/brand team for commercial portfolio use. |
| METRO | Newsroom logos may be used for publishing/private use, but advertising use is prohibited; other protected use can require prior written consent. A personal commercial portfolio is safer to clear in writing. | Ask METRO Corporate Communications for permission. |
| Ursus Breweries / Asahi | Official terms state that third-party use of trademarks and logos is prohibited and that commercial copying/distribution is not permitted under the website licence. | Ask Ursus / Asahi brand or communications team for explicit permission. |

## C. Not yet classified as requiring written approval

The remaining portfolio organizations stay in the factual portfolio registry. Their logo rights should be checked against the specific asset source/brand guidelines before an external downloaded logo is treated as cleared. A factual text reference to the organization and the right to reproduce its graphical trademark are separate issues.

## Implementation rule

- The portfolio registry may contain all factually confirmed organization names.
- User-supplied logo files are stored separately from rights status.
- A visual trademark can be swapped in without changing the portfolio content once its asset and usage status are known.
- Do not interpret a public press-kit download button as a general commercial trademark licence unless the accompanying terms actually say so.
