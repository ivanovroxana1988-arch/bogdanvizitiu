# Site brief

## Objective

Build Bogdan Vizitiu’s authority as a calm, intelligent, warm, experienced,
and pragmatic coach, trainer, entrepreneur, and facilitator. Products should
demonstrate the expertise; they should not reduce the identity to a course
marketplace.

## Audiences

The site serves managers, entrepreneurs, executives, professionals, companies
seeking training or coaching, and individuals considering open courses.

## Positioning pillars

1. Leadership & Teams
2. Negotiation & Sales
3. Networking & Influence
4. Career & Personal Performance

## Experience principles

- Editorial personal brand, not coaching funnel or corporate template
- Typography, approved photography, proportion, and whitespace lead the design
- Warm, premium, masculine, and human
- Restrained palette and motion
- Clear paths for courses, coaching, corporate work, media, resources, and contact
- Romanian is the primary interface language unless the content strategy changes

## Non-negotiable content constraints

- No invented credentials, clients, statistics, testimonials, or appearances
- No generic transformation claims
- No unapproved stock or generated likeness photography
- Anything awaiting confirmation remains unpublished or is visibly identified in
  an authenticated editorial preview—not on the public site

## Required photography

Approved assets should use the filenames mapped in `content/media.json`. Target
crops should cover a portrait-oriented hero, dark portrait, speaking/event,
workshop, coaching conversation, and candid editorial context.

## Launch definition

The public site is not launch-ready until all items in the launch-gaps section of
`IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md` are resolved and every published factual claim has a
source recorded in `SOURCES.md` or the master database.
